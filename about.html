A few snapshots from my life outside of work.

Why I Code

I grew up surrounded by men in technical fields, and instead of being intimidated by that, it pushed me to want to work alongside them. Computer science is an enormous field with a corner of it for almost any interest, and I want more people, especially younger girls, to see that it's a space they belong in too.

Between my work in engineering and my time tutoring, that's the throughline: making tech feel approachable, one person at a time.

Family, Tutoring & Math

As the oldest sister to a lot of younger cousins, I grew up teaching, explaining, and helping the people around me figure things out. It's just always been part of who I am. That's carried directly into my love for tutoring: there's nothing better than watching a concept finally click for someone.

"Math is in my last name."

Vaani Mathur

I use that joke a lot, but it's not entirely a joke. I really do love math, and it's shaped how I think about problem-solving in engineering.

What I'm Into

When I'm not building things, I'm usually deep in a mystery or thriller novel. I love trying to piece together the twist before the book gets there. I'm also a big movie person, and I'll always take an excuse to hang out with friends, whether that's a movie night, a long conversation, or just doing nothing productive together.

I'm also a scrapbooker. I keep one to hold onto precious memories and achievements, and it's the perfect slow, hands-on hobby to balance out the screen time. On the other end of that balance is gaming: you'll usually find me in Fortnite or Marvel Rivals, building away in Minecraft, or unwinding with a relaxing indie game.

Nature & Animals

I could spend hours reading about animals and learning how the natural world works. There's always something new (and a little wild) to discover. That love for animals is a big part of why I adore my two dogs.
